# Community Engagement Plan — Initial Contributor Recruitment

Issue: #8 · Owner: Member 8 · Status: **READY FOR REVIEW (v1.0)**
Depends on: `DECISIONS.md` (D7–D9, D13, D14, D16), `MY_EKITI_STORY_SPEC.md`, `../../12_Ekiti_2056/EKITI_2056_SPEC.md`. Unresolved items are tagged `[OPEN-Mx]`.

## 1. Purpose and goals

This plan covers the Issue #8 task "Design an initial contributor/community engagement plan". It sets out who we want to hear from, how we invite them, what we ask them, and how we keep them informed, from launch through the first months.

**Goals**
1. Get honest, varied stories and visions from all 16 LGAs, not only from the places that are easiest to reach.
2. Reach different age groups (18 and over), professions, backgrounds, and both languages (English and Yoruba).
3. Keep the promise the platform makes: contributors know what happens to what they send, and get updates.
4. Build a habit of contribution that continues after the 30th anniversary.

**Starting targets, PROPOSED `[OPEN-M1]`.** These are guesses, to be replaced with real numbers after the first week of data.

| Measure | First 30 days |
|---|---|
| Submissions received | 100 |
| Published after review | 40 |
| LGAs with at least one submission | 16 of 16 |
| Median time to a moderation decision | Within 3 working days (`CONTRIBUTION_WORKFLOW.md` section 10) |
| Yoruba submissions | Track the share. No target. |

## 2. Principles

1. **Honest expectations.** We say clearly that submissions are reviewed, that review takes time, and that not everything is published.
2. **Contributors stay in control** of their credit, anonymity and removal (`CREDIT_AND_ANONYMITY.md`).
3. **Stories are citizen accounts.** We never promise that a story will become verified history.
4. **Adults only at launch (D9).** Nothing in our outreach targets under-18s, including school pupils. Old students' associations and alumni are welcome.
5. **Privacy first (D14).** People are sent to the form. We do not collect stories, names or contact details in comments, direct messages or group chats. Assisted contribution (section 8) is the only exception.
6. **Non-partisan.** We invite ideas about Ekiti's future and do not carry campaign material (`MODERATION_GUIDE.md`, check 13).
7. **Respect** for elders, tradition and differences of opinion. Never mock dialect, spelling or a mix of languages.
8. **No fabricated content.** We never post invented stories, quotes or testimonials. Any example we show is either a real approved submission or is clearly marked as an example.
9. **Independent.** The platform is not an official government platform, and is never presented as one.

## 4. Key messages

**Say**
- "Share your story of Ekiti. Share your vision for Ekiti in 2056."
- "You choose how you are credited, including anonymously."
- "Your story is published as your own account. Facts in it may be checked."
- "Send it in English or Yoruba."
- "You must be 18 or over to contribute for now."
- "Everything is reviewed before it is published."
- "This is free, and takes about 10 minutes."

**Don't say**
- Anything suggesting everything will be published.
- "Verified by us" about a story. Only individual facts can be verified.
- That this is a government project, or an official record.
- Anything promising rewards, prizes or fame.
- Anything that pressures someone to share a painful memory.

Every public message uses the wording approved by Member 1 `[OPEN-M1]`. Yoruba versions are written or approved by Faith Ogunlade, the confirmed Yoruba-capable reviewer (D13).

## 8. Ambassadors and assisted contribution — PROPOSED `[OPEN-M1]`

**Ambassadors.** One or two adult volunteers per LGA who share the call, answer questions about how it works, and help people who ask for help with the form.

Ambassadors:
- do not moderate, review or approve anything;
- never see anyone's identity or contact details, other than the person they are helping, at the time;
- never promise that something will be published;
- follow the moderator conduct rules in `MODERATION_GUIDE.md` section 7;
- are not paid, and are not the public face of the platform.

**Assisted contribution.** Some people, especially elders, will prefer to tell their story aloud, or have no smartphone. The ambassador helps them submit:
1. The contributor is present throughout, and decides everything.
2. The ambassador reads the consent and privacy notice aloud, in the contributor's language.
3. The ambassador types what the contributor says, then reads it back for the contributor to confirm.
4. The contact details entered are the contributor's own, never the ambassador's.
5. No audio or video is recorded at launch (images only, `MY_EKITI_STORY_SPEC.md` section 6).
6. Any paper notes are destroyed once the submission has been sent.
7. If the contributor changes their mind at any point, nothing is submitted.

The contributor is treated exactly like any other, and the submission goes through the same review.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| Few submissions from some LGAs | Track coverage weekly. Use ambassadors and assisted sessions. Do not fill gaps with invented content. |
| Political content after the election period | Follow the campaign-material rule (`MODERATION_GUIDE.md`, check 13). Keep prompts about ideas and development. |
| Spam or a flood of submissions | Form protection (Member 2). Moderation triage daily. Do not reply to spam. |
| Moderation backlog | Track time to decision. Bring in a second moderator `[OPEN-M1]`. Tell contributors honestly when review is slow. |
| Misinformation presented as fact | Citizen-account labels, verification of factual claims, and correction notes (`CORRECTIONS_AND_CLARIFICATIONS.md`). |
| Harassment in comments on our channels | Comment rules, hiding or removing abusive comments, and blocking repeat offenders. Threats go to Member 1. |
| Painful or sensitive stories | Never push. Handle with care, use the sensitive flag, and hand distressing cases to Member 1 (`MODERATION_GUIDE.md` sections 6 and 7). |
| A contributor regrets sharing | Removal the same day once confirmed (`CORRECTIONS_AND_CLARIFICATIONS.md` section 7). |
| Privacy incident, for example identity shown by mistake | Unpublish at once, tell Member 1, and follow the incident steps from the privacy policy `[OPEN-M1]`. |
| Contributors do not understand the review process | Plain explainer, the confirmation message, and status updates. |
