# Ogun Onire Festival

## Documented Facts

Ire-Ekiti, in Oye LGA, is one of the most prominent Ogun-worship centres in Yorubaland, and hosts an annual festival honouring the deity. In August 2020, Ekiti State's COVID-19 order to suspend traditional festivals led the Onire of Ire, Oba Victor Bobade, to ask festival adherents to shelve that year's celebration; this triggered a violent clash in the town in which two people were reportedly killed and the monarch's vehicle was set ablaze, prompting a state police deployment and the declaration of 15 wanted suspects.

## Traditional / Community Account

Yoruba tradition holds that Ogun (god of iron and war) was once the earthly king of Ire before disappearing into the earth ("wo ile sun") at Ire-Ekiti rather than dying, and that he promised to help those who call on his name — this is the religious basis for the festival, not a historical event.
