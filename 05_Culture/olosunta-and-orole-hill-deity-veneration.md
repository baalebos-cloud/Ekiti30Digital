# Olosunta and Orole Hill Deity Veneration

## Traditional / Community Account

The twin hills overlooking Ikere-Ekiti, Olosunta and Orole, are each treated as the seat of a resident deity; worshippers credit the deities with granting children to childless women and with protecting the town in past wars. This belief underlies the annual Olosunta festival (see Festivals).
