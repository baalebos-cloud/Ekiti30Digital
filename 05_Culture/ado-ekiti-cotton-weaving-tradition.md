# Ado-Ekiti Cotton Weaving Tradition

## Documented Facts

A long-standing local tradition of cotton weaving and textile production in Ado-Ekiti, which fed into the establishment of a large textile mill in the town in 1967. Britannica cites this among Ado-Ekiti's defining traditional industries, alongside shoemaking and pottery.
