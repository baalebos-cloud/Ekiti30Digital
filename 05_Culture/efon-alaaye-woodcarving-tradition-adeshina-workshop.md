# Efon-Alaaye Woodcarving Tradition (Adeshina Workshop)

## Documented Facts

In the early 20th century, Efon-Alaaye was the leading centre of wood carving in southern Ekiti, anchored by the Adeshina family workshop, which produced palace veranda posts, doors, Ibeji (twin) figures and carved wooden Epa masks, as well as beadwork including beaded crowns. The workshop's best-known carver, Agbonbiofe (c.1880–1945), carved over twenty veranda posts for the Alaaye's palace after an early-1900s fire; his works, documented by scholars including William Fagg, are now held in major museum and private collections worldwide.
