# Olowe of Ise Sculpture Legacy

## Documented Facts

Olowe of Ise (d. 1934) was a master carver whose veranda posts, palace doors and bowls are considered among the finest examples of Yoruba sculpture, held in museums across the US and Europe. Ekiti State's plan to build a museum on his home compound is explicitly framed as an effort to bring recognition of his legacy back to Ise-Ekiti.
