# Fajuyi Memorial Park

## Documented Facts

A triangular park in central Ado-Ekiti built around the burial site of Lt. Col. Francis Adekunle Fajuyi, former military governor of Nigeria's old Western Region, who was killed alongside Head of State Gen. Aguiyi-Ironsi in the July 1966 counter-coup after refusing to abandon his guest to the coup plotters. He was given a heroic burial. The park now also hosts the 'Great Fajuyi Hall', built as part of Udiroko festival development projects.
