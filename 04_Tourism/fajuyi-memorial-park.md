# Fajuyi Memorial Park

## Summary

Fajuyi Memorial Park is a memorial and tourist site in Ado-Ekiti associated with Colonel Adekunle Fajuyi.

## Facts

- Fajuyi Memorial Park is a triangular park in Ado-Ekiti where the late Colonel Adekunle Fajuyi was buried. [S1]
- The Ekiti State Government tourism page identifies Adekunle Fajuyi as a governor of the old Western State and states that he died during the 1966 coup and was accorded a heroic burial. [S1]

## Evidence Status
