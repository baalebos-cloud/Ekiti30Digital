# Ero Dam & Lake

## Documented Facts

A man-made dam and lake on a river near Ikun-Ekiti; originally designed to cover about 5 km but grew to roughly 11 km, it supplies pipe-borne water to Oye, Ido-Osi and Moba LGAs, and supports fishing (by canoe and motorised boat) and irrigation farming.
