# Olowe of Ise Museum (under construction)

## Documented Facts

A museum announced by the Ekiti State Government to be built on Olowe of Ise's original compound, intended to house his works (many currently abroad) plus educational programming on Yoruba art. Backed by a dedicated Olowe of Ise Arts Foundation (chaired by Senator Babafemi Ojudu) with a 13-member Board of Trustees including Yemisi Shyllon (Shyllon Museum), Nike Okundaye (Nike Art Gallery) and curator Tunde Odunlade; the National Commission for Museums and Monuments has also backed the preservation effort. A Lagos fundraiser was held to finance construction, artwork acquisition and running costs.
