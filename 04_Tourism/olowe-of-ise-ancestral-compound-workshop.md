# Olowe of Ise Ancestral Compound & Workshop

## Documented Facts

The house and original workshop of master woodcarver Olowe of Ise (d. 1934) in Ise-Ekiti, designated by the Ekiti State Government as the site of a planned museum. Olowe's carved veranda posts, doors and bowls are held in major Western museums; the state government has cited the dispersal of his work abroad as motivation for the museum project.
