# Erin Ayonigba Sacred Fish River

## Documented Facts

A river home to a colony of catfish regarded as sacred; the fish are traditionally not eaten and killing or eating them is forbidden.

## Traditional / Community Account

The sacredness of the fish, and the taboo against eating them, is a traditional/religious belief, not an independently verifiable fact.
