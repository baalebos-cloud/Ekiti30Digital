# Ewi of Ado-Ekiti Palace

## Documented Facts

The royal palace of the Ewi (paramount traditional ruler) of Ado-Ekiti. Ado-Ekiti's status as the state capital gives the Ewi's throne particular prominence; the palace is the venue for the annual Udiroko festival and other state ceremonies, and its grounds have seen recent additions such as a modern architectural amphitheatre.

## Traditional / Community Account

Government press material describes the palace as an architectural showcase and a de facto mini-museum of royal artefacts and regalia; this characterisation has not been independently documented or inventoried.
