# Ikogosi Warm Springs

## Summary

Ikogosi Warm Springs is a tourism site at Ikogosi-Ekiti in Ekiti West Local Government Area. The site is known for warm and cold springs that flow from separate sources and meet while retaining different thermal characteristics.

## Facts

- Ikogosi Warm Springs is located at Ikogosi-Ekiti in Ekiti West Local Government Area of Ekiti State. [S1]
- Warm and cold water flow from separate sources at Ikogosi and meet in a pool while retaining different thermal characteristics. [S1]
- The Baptist Mission established a youth and conference centre near the warm spring area in the early 1950s. [S1]
- In 1978, Ondo State acquired the tourist centre from the Baptist Mission for tourism development. [S1]

## Traditional / Community Account

The Ekiti State Government tourism page records a traditional account in which the warm and cold springs are described as two wives of the same husband who became springs following a dispute. This is recorded as oral tradition and must not be presented by Ask Ekiti as independently verified historical fact. [S1]
