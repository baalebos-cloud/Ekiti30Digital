# Ikogosi Warm Springs

## Documented Facts

A naturally occurring warm spring and a cold spring flow from separate sources on adjoining hills and meet in one pool, each reportedly keeping its own temperature. A Baptist mission ran a youth centre on the site from the early 1950s; the state government took the site over for tourism development in 1978, and a resort/conference facility has operated on the hill above the springs since.

## Traditional / Community Account

Local oral tradition explains the two springs as a quarrelling husband and wife who were turned to water. The warm spring's water is also popularly credited with therapeutic/healing properties by visitors and locals.
