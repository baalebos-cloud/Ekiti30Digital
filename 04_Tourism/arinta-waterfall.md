# Arinta Waterfall

## Summary

Arinta Waterfall is a natural waterfall and internationally designated wetland site at Ipole-Iloro in Ekiti West Local Government Area.

## Facts

- Arinta Waterfall is located at Ipole-Iloro in Ekiti State, within Ekiti West Local Government Area. [S1]
- Ramsar lists Arinta Waterfall as Site No. 2565, designated on 1 March 2024, with an area of about 181.41 hectares. [S1]
- The Ramsar record describes Arinta as a stream cascading down rocky hills into pools surrounded by natural forest vegetation. [S1]
- The Ramsar information sheet describes the waterfall as cascading down a steep escarpment in three distinct stages, with clear pools at the base. [S1]

## Research Notes / Excluded Claims

Earlier project research recorded community claims about a seventeenth-century origin and healing properties of the water. Those claims are not established by SRC-047 and are therefore excluded from Ask Ekiti factual retrieval until suitable evidence is registered.

## Evidence Status

The Ramsar record is an official international wetland record. The Source Inventory currently remains at Needs review, while this document is separately approved for Ask Ekiti publication.
