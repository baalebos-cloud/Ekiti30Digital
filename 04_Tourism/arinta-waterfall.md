# Arinta Waterfall

## Documented Facts

A multi-tier waterfall cascading roughly 50 metres down rocky hills into a forest pool, about a 15-minute drive from Ikogosi. The surrounding wetland is registered as Ramsar Site No. 2565 (Nigeria) for its biodiversity value.

## Traditional / Community Account

Community elders date the falls' existence to at least the 17th century and attribute healing properties to the water; locals report the falls have up to six or seven distinct tiers.
