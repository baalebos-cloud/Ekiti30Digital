## File Naming

Photographs retain their current repository filenames and use the canonical three-digit EK record ID at the beginning of each filename.

Examples:

```text
EK-002 bawa first gov.jpg
EK-020 Ikogosi-Warm-Springs ent.jpeg
EK-039 udiroko fest.jpg
EK-046 tunji oyeb past gov.jpeg
```

The EK IDs are normalized to the three-digit format, including `EK-039` and `EK-046`.

## Historical Media Metadata

The canonical metadata for these photographs is maintained in:

`16_Media/Historical/EKITI30_Historical_Media_Tracker_UPDATED.csv`

The canonical tracker records the related EK timeline ID, asset description, source information, source URL, creator or photographer where known, copyright/reuse-permission status, attribution requirements, verification status, repository filename and research limitations.

The photographs in this folder do **not** maintain a separate metadata tracker. This avoids duplicate or conflicting historical-media records and keeps provenance and rights information within the project's existing `16_Media/Historical/` workflow.

## Rights and Verification

Finding, downloading or hosting an image does not by itself establish permission to reuse or republish it.

Where copyright, licence, ownership or reuse permission has not been established, the canonical tracker keeps the asset at `Needs verification` or the applicable unresolved rights status. These photographs should therefore not be treated as publication-cleared assets until the required provenance and rights checks have been completed.

Attribution requirements and known creator/owner information are also recorded in the canonical tracker.

## Image Treatment

Image treatment information is recorded with the relevant asset notes in the canonical historical-media tracker.

The Mohammed Inua Bawa photograph (`EK-002`) included in this folder is the **original downloaded image**. The AI-enhanced version produced separately during research was **not used in this collection**.

If any photograph is cleaned, cropped, restored or otherwise enhanced in the future, that treatment should be recorded separately from its original source and should not be presented as an unaltered historical photograph.
