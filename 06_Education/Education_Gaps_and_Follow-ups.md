# Education --- Gaps and Follow-ups

1.  **1996--2026 enrolment/literacy time series:** no complete
    continuous series has yet been assembled; Ministry/SUBEB/UBEC
    archival data is still needed.
2.  **ASUU/industrial-action history:** compile Ekiti-specific
    disruption records for EKSU, FUOYE and other affected institutions
    from union/university archives.
3.  **Legacy secondary schools:** Christ's School is documented, but a
    fuller pre-1996 mission/government-school inventory remains open.
4.  **Oyebanji-era expenditure/recruitment figures:** ₦14bn for 203
    secondary schools, 2,000+ teachers and ₦1.2bn exam-fee figures
    remain ATTRIBUTED until budget, programme or audit documents
    independently corroborate them.
5.  **Ekiti Knowledge Zone:** the AfDB US\$80m financing is now
    independently VERIFIED.
6.  **"Highest number of professors" claim:** no authoritative national
    professors-by-state dataset was located; keep only as a reputation
    claim.
7.  **Crown Polytechnic founding year:** existence/location/programmes
    are verified; founding date needs NBTE/regulatory or archival
    primary evidence.
8.  **Annual School Census:** use the official table totals. One
    narrative paragraph contains inconsistent gender figures and should
    not be copied as verified data.
9.  **Yoruba review and Verification Lead sign-off:** still required
