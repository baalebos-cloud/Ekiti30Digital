# 2023--2024 Ekiti State Annual School Census

**Primary source:** Ekiti State Ministry of Education official Annual
School Census PDF\
https://ekitistate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/Edu/2023-2024%20Ekiti%20ASCE.pdf

## Verified ECCDE / Pre-Primary snapshot

-   Total ECCDE/pre-primary schools: **1,499**
-   Public schools: **920 (61.4%)**
-   Private schools: **579 (38.6%)**
-   Total ECCDE/pre-primary enrolment: **59,997**
-   Public enrolment: **32,847**
-   Private enrolment: **27,150**
-   Statewide male enrolment: **29,742**
-   Statewide female enrolment: **30,255**
-   Female share of total enrolment: **50.4%**
-   Ado-Ekiti has **203 private ECCDE/pre-primary schools**, the largest
    private-school concentration shown in the relevant table.
-   Public-school ECCDE/pre-primary enrolment in Ado LGA is **3,877**.

## Data-quality note

The official PDF contains an internal inconsistency in the narrative
below its public-school age/gender table: that paragraph reports
male/female figures that do not reconcile with the table total of
32,847. For the knowledge base, use the tabulated figures and the
statewide public/private summary: **29,742 male + 30,255 female = 59,997
total**.

Do not reproduce the inconsistent narrative figures as verified
statistics without a clarification from the Ministry.
