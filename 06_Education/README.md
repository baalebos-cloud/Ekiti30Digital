# 06_Education --- EKITI@30 DIGITAL

This folder contains the research-ready education knowledge-base package
for Ekiti State, with emphasis on the 1996--2026 period and a small
number of essential pre-1996 heritage institutions.

## Contents

-   `timeline/education_timeline_1996-2026.md` --- discrete timeline
    claims with evidence status.
-   `institutions/` --- institutional metadata, sources and
    media-permission notes.
-   `policy/free_education_and_levy_history.md` --- levy, free-education
    and school-feeding history.
-   `data/2023-2024_annual_school_census_summary.md` --- verified ASC
    snapshot and data-quality note.
-   `Education_Gaps_and_Follow-ups.md` --- unresolved research items.
-   `kb_manifest_education_rows.csv` --- rows to append to the root
    project manifest.
-   `INTEGRATION_NOTES_FOR_MEMBER4.md` --- GitHub/integration guidance.

## Verification approach

Priority was given to: 1. Ekiti State Government and Ministry of
Education records. 2. Federal agencies and international development
institutions (UBEC, Federal Ministry of Education, World Bank, African
Development Bank). 3. Universities/polytechnics' own institutional
records. 4. Reputable independent press and fact-checking organisations
for corroboration and disputed claims.

Government self-reported financial/performance figures are not
automatically treated as independently verified. Where independent
programme/audit evidence was not found, the timeline retains
`ATTRIBUTED`.

## Important corrections in this research pass

-   ECCDE/pre-primary total enrolment is **59,997**, not 32,847. The
    latter is the **public-school** component; private enrolment is
    27,150.
-   Ekiti Kete Meals was prepared/announced in early 2019 and formally
    launched in May 2019.
-   AfDB's **US\$80m Ekiti Knowledge Zone financing** is independently
    verified.
-   FUOYE, ABUAD and BOUESTI records now rely primarily on institutional
    sources.
-   Ekiti State Polytechnic's statutory name is supported by the 2021
    state law.
-   Venite University's portal identifies 2022 as its establishment
    year.
-   Crown Polytechnic's founding year is left unresolved rather than
    guessed.
