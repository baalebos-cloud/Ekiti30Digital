# Free Education and Education Development Levy History

## 2015 --- Education Development Levy

Reporting from 2018 consistently states that the Ayodele Fayose
administration had introduced the Education Development Levy in 2015:
₦500 per term for primary pupils and ₦1,000 per term for secondary
students.

## October 2018 --- Levy abolished; free and compulsory education order

Governor Kayode Fayemi abolished the levy after returning to office. On
24 October 2018 he signed an executive order providing for free and
compulsory education in government-owned primary and secondary schools
and prohibiting unauthorised fees.

## 2019 --- Ekiti Kete Meals

Early-2019 reporting described preparations for Ekiti Kete Meals. The
Office of the Vice President later documented the formal launch/flag-off
in May 2019 under the National Home-Grown School Feeding Programme.

## 2020 fact-checking context

Africa Check found that Ekiti was not the lowest South-West state for
2018 school enrolment: primary net enrolment was reported at 63.9% and
junior-secondary net enrolment at 37.8%. The later claim that enrolment
had risen above 75% was judged unproven because comparable supporting
data was not provided.
