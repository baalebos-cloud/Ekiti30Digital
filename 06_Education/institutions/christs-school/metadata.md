# Christ's School, Ado-Ekiti

-   Founded in 1933 by Archdeacon Henry Dallimore as Ekiti Central
    School.
-   Moved to the Agidimo Hills site in 1936, when the school received
    the name Christ's School.
-   The school is retained as a pre-1996 heritage institution because of
    its major place in Ekiti's education history.
-   Cross-reference: EK-EDU-002.
