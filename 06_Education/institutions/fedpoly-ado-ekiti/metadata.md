# Federal Polytechnic, Ado-Ekiti

-   Listed by Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Education as an approved
    federal polytechnic.
-   Federal Ministry of Education lists its establishment year as 1977.
-   The institution's historical account records relocation before its
    permanent base in Ado-Ekiti.
