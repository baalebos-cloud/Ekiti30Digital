# Federal University Oye-Ekiti (FUOYE)

-   Established by the Federal Government in 2011.
-   Operates a multi-campus structure centred on Oye-Ekiti and
    Ikole-Ekiti.
-   Institutional records identify the university as part of the federal
    expansion intended to improve access to tertiary education.
-   Cross-reference: EK-EDU-004.
