## Summary

Afe Babalola University (ABUAD) is a private university in Ado-Ekiti. This record preserves only institutional-history facts supported by the registered official ABUAD source.

## Facts

- Afe Babalola University was founded by Aare Afe Babalola. [S1]
- The first sod was turned at the university site on 1 October 2008. [S1]
- An assessment team from the National Universities Commission visited the completed university campus in May 2009. [S1]
