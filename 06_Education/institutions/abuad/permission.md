# Media Permission --- Afe Babalola University (ABUAD), Ado-Ekiti

No media asset is included in this knowledge-base record. Any photograph
or scan used in the public EKITI@30 platform must be logged through the
historical-media workflow with source, creator/owner where known,
date/context, licence or permission status, and attribution
requirements. An image being visible on an institutional website does
not by itself establish reuse permission.
