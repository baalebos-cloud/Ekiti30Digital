# BOUESTI, Ikere-Ekiti

-   Established on 16 October 2020 following upgrade from the former
    College of Education.
-   Received its operational licence from the National Universities
    Commission on 6 November 2020.
-   Pioneer principal officers were appointed on 6 January 2021.
-   NUC approved 45 degree programmes on 31 March 2021.
-   Pioneer students resumed on 12 July 2021.
-   Cross-reference: EK-EDU-006.
