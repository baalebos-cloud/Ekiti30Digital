# Ekiti State College of Health Sciences and Technology (COHESTI), Ijero-Ekiti
- Also referred to as EKSCOTECH, ESCOHSTI, or ESCOHST across its own sites and press —
  all the same institution.
- **Establishment date is disputed across sources and unresolved this pass:**
  Wikipedia and the college's UTME/admissions pages give **2010**, under Ekiti State
  Law No. 3 of 2010; the college's own "About" page on its main site gives
  **2001**. Do not ingest either date as VERIFIED until the discrepancy is resolved
  against a primary legal/gazette document.
- Trains mid-level health personnel: Nursing, Dental Therapy/Health, Medical
  Laboratory Technology, Paramedicine Science, Health Information Technology,
  Environmental Health, Community/Public Health, Pharmacy technicians.
- Regulated by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE) and the Ekiti State
  Ministry of Education.
- Initially operated as a monotechnic; an 8-member committee (constituted 15 March
  2024) recommended conversion to a polytechnic under Gov. Biodun Oyebanji, with
  final approval granted 19 February 2025.
- Offers B.Sc. top-up degree pathways via affiliation with Kwara State University
  (KWASU) per the college's own site — not independently corroborated this pass.
