# Ekiti 2056 — Citizen Visions

Owner: **Member 8 — Community, Media & Verification Lead**
Related issue: **#8 — Design citizen stories and Ekiti 2056 contribution workflow**
Status: **Ready for review (v1.0)**

## Purpose

Ekiti 2056 is the citizen vision space for ideas, hopes, proposals and aspirations for the next 30 years of Ekiti.

A vision is not a historical fact. Review focuses on safety, relevance, clarity, respect, categorisation and publication suitability.

## Shared workflow

Moderation, statuses, credit, anonymity and corrections are shared with My Ekiti Story. See `11_Citizen_Stories/workflow/`.
