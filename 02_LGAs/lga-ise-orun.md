## Summary
Ise/Orun is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ise-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Communities and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ise/Orun Local Government Area's headquarters is Ise-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ise/Orun LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.465, longitude 5.423. [S2]
- Ise/Orun LGA includes the communities of Ise-Ekiti, Orun-Ekiti. [S1]
