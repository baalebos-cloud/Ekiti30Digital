## Summary
Ikere is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ikere-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Institution, landmarks and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ikere Local Government Area's headquarters is Ikere-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ikere LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.498, longitude 5.232. [S2]
- Ikere LGA is home to Olosunta Hills. [S1]
- Ikere LGA is home to Orole Hills. [S1]
- Bamidele Olumilua University of Education, Science and Technology is located in Ikere LGA. [S1]
