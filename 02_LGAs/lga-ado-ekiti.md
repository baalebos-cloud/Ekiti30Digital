## Summary
Ado Ekiti is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ado-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Coordinates and listed places require independent review before publication

## Facts
- Ado Ekiti Local Government Area's headquarters is Ado-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ado Ekiti LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.621111, longitude 5.221389. [S2]
- Ado Ekiti LGA is home to Fajuyi Memorial Park. [S1]
- Ado Ekiti LGA is home to Ewi of Ado-Ekiti's Palace. [S1]
- Ekiti State University is located in Ado Ekiti LGA. [S1]
- Afe Babalola University is located in Ado Ekiti LGA. [S1]
- Federal Polytechnic Ado-Ekiti is located in Ado Ekiti LGA. [S1]
