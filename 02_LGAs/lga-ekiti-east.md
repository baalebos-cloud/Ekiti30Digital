## Summary
Ekiti East is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Omuo-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Communities and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ekiti East Local Government Area's headquarters is Omuo-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ekiti East LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.75833, longitude 5.72227. [S2]
- Ekiti East LGA includes the communities of Omuo-Ekiti, Isinbode-Ekiti, Ilasa-Ekiti. [S1]
