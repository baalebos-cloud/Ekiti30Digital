## Summary
Ido/Osi is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ido-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Institution and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ido/Osi Local Government Area's headquarters is Ido-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ido/Osi LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.846, longitude 5.183. [S2]
- Ido/Osi LGA includes the communities of Ido-Ekiti, Ifaki-Ekiti, Usi-Ekiti, Ayetoro-Ekiti. [S1]
- Federal Teaching Hospital Ido-Ekiti is located in Ido/Osi LGA. [S1]
