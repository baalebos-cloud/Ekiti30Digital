## Summary
Ijero is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ijero-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Institution and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ijero Local Government Area's headquarters is Ijero-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ijero LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.815, longitude 5.067. [S2]
- Ekiti State College of Health Sciences and Technology, Ijero-Ekiti is located in Ijero LGA. [S1]
