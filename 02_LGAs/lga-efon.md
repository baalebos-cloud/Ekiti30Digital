## Summary
Efon is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Efon-Alaaye. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Landmarks and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Efon Local Government Area's headquarters is Efon-Alaaye. [S1]
- Efon LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.65649, longitude 4.92235. [S2]
- Efon LGA is home to Efon hills. [S1]
