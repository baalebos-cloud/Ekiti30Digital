## Summary
Irepodun/Ifelodun is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Igede-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. FUTES in Iyin-Ekiti is supported by an official FUTA publication. Its operational details and exact campus coordinates still require independent verification.

## Facts
- Irepodun/Ifelodun Local Government Area's headquarters is Igede-Ekiti. [S1]
- Irepodun/Ifelodun LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.668, longitude 5.126. [S2]
- Irepodun/Ifelodun LGA includes the communities of Igede-Ekiti, Iyin-Ekiti, Iworoko-Ekiti. [S1]
- Federal University of Technology and Environmental Sciences (FUTES), Iyin-Ekiti is located in Irepodun/Ifelodun LGA. [S3]
