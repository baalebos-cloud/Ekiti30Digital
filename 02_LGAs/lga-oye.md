## Summary
Oye is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Oye-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Institution, communities and coordinates require independent verification. Ekiti State Polytechnic, Isan-Ekiti, is supported by the institution's official website; its exact campus coordinates still require independent verification.

## Facts
- Oye Local Government Area's headquarters is Oye-Ekiti. [S1]
- Oye LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.8, longitude 5.333. [S2]
- Oye LGA includes the communities of Oye-Ekiti, Ayede-Ekiti, Itapa-Ekiti, Ilupeju-Ekiti, Isan-Ekiti. [S1]
- Federal University Oye-Ekiti is located in Oye LGA. [S3]
- Ekiti State Polytechnic, Isan-Ekiti is located in Oye LGA. [S3]
