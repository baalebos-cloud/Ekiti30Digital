## Summary
Ekiti South-West is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ilawe-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Headquarters information is supported by the official Ekiti State directory. Coordinates and landmark details remain pending.

## Facts
- Ekiti South-West Local Government Area's headquarters is Ilawe-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ekiti South-West LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.6, longitude 5.1. [S2]
- Ekiti South-West LGA is home to Alawe of Ilawe's Palace. [S1]
