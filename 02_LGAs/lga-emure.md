## Summary
Emure is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Emure-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Landmark and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Emure Local Government Area's headquarters is Emure-Ekiti. [S1]
- Emure LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.45, longitude 5.466667. [S2]
- Emure LGA is home to Elemure of Emure's Palace. [S1]
