## Summary
Ekiti West is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Aramoko-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Tourism sites and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ekiti West Local Government Area's headquarters is Aramoko-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ekiti West LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.711, longitude 5.044. [S2]
- Ekiti West LGA includes the communities of Aramoko-Ekiti, Ikogosi-Ekiti, Ipole-Iloro, Erijiyan-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ekiti West LGA is home to Ikogosi Warm Springs. [S1]
- Ekiti West LGA is home to Arinta Waterfall. [S1]
