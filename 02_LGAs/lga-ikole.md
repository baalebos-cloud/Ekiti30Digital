## Summary
Ikole is one of the 16 Local Government Areas of Ekiti State, with its headquarters at Ikole-Ekiti. This entry is a draft: coordinates, place and institution listings are pending independent review. Communities and coordinates require independent verification

## Facts
- Ikole Local Government Area's headquarters is Ikole-Ekiti. [S1]
- Ikole LGA's headquarters (headquarters town centre) is at approximately latitude 7.797, longitude 5.514. [S2]
- Ikole LGA includes the communities of Ikole-Ekiti, Odo-Ayedun-Ekiti. [S1]
