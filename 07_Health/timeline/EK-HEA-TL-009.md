# Ekiti State Health Insurance Scheme law

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-009
    **Period:** 2016
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    Ekiti enacted the Health Insurance Scheme Law in 2016, creating the legal foundation for a state insurance mechanism intended to expand equitable access and financial risk protection.
