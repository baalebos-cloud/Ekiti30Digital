# Current UHC/PHC strengthening and major hospital expansion

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-017
    **Period:** 2025-2026
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    The 2025-26 period includes reported revitalisation of 103 PHCs under the World Bank-supported IMPACT project, continuing Ulerawa, health-worker investment, EKSUTH's 80-bed multipurpose building, power and water enabling works, upgrades of PHCs to general hospitals at Ikogosi, Awo and Ipao, and new hospital construction/equipment. By September 2026, the State Executive Council had approved ₦6.766 billion for new hospital buildings at the three upgraded locations, with ten-month completion targets.
