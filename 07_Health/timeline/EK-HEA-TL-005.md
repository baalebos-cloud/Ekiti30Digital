# Fayemi-era free-care, maternal health and hospital-renovation expansion

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-005
    **Period:** 2010-2014
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    The Fayemi administration reported a broad health agenda including free services for pregnant women, children under five, persons with disabilities and older citizens; mobile free-health missions; maternal-health supplies; ambulance expansion; workforce training; health-data strengthening; and renovation of secondary hospitals. In 2013, government formally launched the Operation Renovate All Hospitals programme for 18 general hospitals and announced two new hospitals.
