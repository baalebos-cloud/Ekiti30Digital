# BHCPF implementation and PHC financing

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-013
    **Period:** 2021
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    The state insurance programme reports BHCPF as launched in 2021 to strengthen primary healthcare, provide a basic minimum package of services and support emergency medical treatment. Ekiti's PHC financing architecture has subsequently been tied to ward/facility-level implementation and broader UHC objectives.
