# Early consolidation of state secondary health administration

**Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-002
**Period:** 1996-2000
**Sector:** Health

## Summary
The first years after state creation were characterized by consolidation of hospitals, staffing and administrative systems inherited from the former Ondo State structure. The current Hospitals Management Board describes responsibility for secondary hospitals, staffing, infrastructure, medicines, nursing, data and supervision.
