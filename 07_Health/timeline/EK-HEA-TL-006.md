# Commissioning of 300-bed Oba Adejugbe Hospital

**Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-006
**Period:** 2014
**Sector:** Health

## Summary
A 300-bed Oba Adejugbe General Hospital in Ado-Ekiti was inaugurated in October 2014. The project represented a major secondary-care infrastructure investment during the 2010-2014 period.
