# Grassroots/mobile free-health outreach under the Fayose administration

**Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-007
**Period:** 2015-2018
**Sector:** Health

## Summary
The period included continuation of free-care and outreach approaches, including the Ilera Ayo programme described in 2018 as taking medical services to homes and farmsteads, especially for people unable to travel to urban facilities.
