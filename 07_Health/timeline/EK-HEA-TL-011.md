# COVID-19 emergency response architecture

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-011
    **Period:** 2020
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    Ekiti established a 40-person COVID-19 task force, activated facilities and border/transport screening, reactivated surveillance teams, established a 24/7 Emergency Operations Center and hotlines, deployed ambulances, and later launched a GIS-based COVID-19 Response Hub. The state reported its first COVID-19 case on 18 March 2020.
