# Renewed health-system rebuilding and medical workforce strengthening

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-010
    **Period:** 2018-2019
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    The Fayemi administration's return period included free-health missions, hospital-bill support, efforts to strengthen PHC governance, partnership with Federal Teaching Hospital Ido-Ekiti, and support for the EKSU College of Medicine. In 2019, 43 pioneer EKSU medical graduates were inducted after full accreditation, with the state announcing plans to retain interested graduates.
