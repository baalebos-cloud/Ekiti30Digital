# Primary Health Care Development Agency legal framework

**Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-008
**Period:** 2012
**Sector:** Health

## Summary
The Ekiti State Primary Health Care Development Agency Law, 2012 established the agency and a governance structure including primary-health-care zones, local government health authorities, ward health committees and facility health committees.
