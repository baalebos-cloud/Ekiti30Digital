# EKSUTH established in law

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-004
    **Period:** 2008
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    EKSUTH's institutional history states that the teaching hospital was established by an Ekiti State House of Assembly edict assented to in April 2008, evolving from the former State Specialist Hospital, Ado-Ekiti. It became a major state tertiary-care and health-training institution.
