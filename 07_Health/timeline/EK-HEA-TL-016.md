# Secondary-hospital renovation, equipment and pharmaceutical-supply modernization

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-016
    **Period:** 2024-2025
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    Government approved major renovation and equipment work for nine general hospitals and related facilities, while the health sector also upgraded the state drug warehouse to a pharma-grade facility. The 2024-25 period also saw the launch/expansion of PHC revitalisation and Ulerawa-related interventions.
