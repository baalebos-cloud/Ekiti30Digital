# Creation of Ekiti State and transition of health administration

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-001
    **Period:** 1996
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    The creation of Ekiti State in 1996 placed state-owned health facilities serving the new state under a distinct Ekiti administrative structure. The state Hospitals Management Board records a succession of senior health administrators beginning in November 1996, providing evidence of an institutional health-management structure from the state's earliest period.
