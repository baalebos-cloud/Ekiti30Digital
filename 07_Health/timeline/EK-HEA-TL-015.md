# Conversion of Ikole Specialist Hospital to Federal Medical Centre

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-015
    **Period:** 2024
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    In July 2024, the Federal Government and Ekiti State Government signed an MoU to convert and upgrade the Ikole Specialist Hospital to a Federal Medical Centre. The institution subsequently began operating as FMC Ikole-Ekiti and reports provision of increasingly complex specialist procedures.
