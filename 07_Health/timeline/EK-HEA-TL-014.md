# Oyebanji administration: Ulerawa, hospital modernization and PHC expansion

    **Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-014
    **Period:** 2022-2024
    **Sector:** Health

    ## Summary
    The current administration expanded Ulerawa/health-insurance-supported free services, renovated secondary hospitals, invested in equipment and power, strengthened medicines supply, and developed projects at EKSUTH. By July 2024, government reported more than 429,000 residents had accessed Ulerawa-supported free medical services through 177 health centres.
