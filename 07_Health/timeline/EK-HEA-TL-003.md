# Ikole hospital upgraded to State Specialist Hospital

**Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-003
**Period:** 2001
**Sector:** Health

## Summary
The present Federal Medical Centre Ikole history records that in 2001 the former General Hospital Ikole was redesignated as State Specialist Hospital, expanding its specialist-health role before its later federal conversion in 2024.
