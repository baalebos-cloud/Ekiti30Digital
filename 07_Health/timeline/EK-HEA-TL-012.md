# Launch of the Ekiti State Health Insurance Scheme

**Document ID:** EK-HEA-TL-012
**Period:** 2020
**Sector:** Health

## Summary
The state health-insurance authority reports that EKHIS was officially launched on 10 July 2020, beginning with the Equity Health Plan. The scheme subsequently incorporated BHCPF and the Ulerawa programme as major interventions.
