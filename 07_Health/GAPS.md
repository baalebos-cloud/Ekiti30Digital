# Evidence Gaps / Items Requiring Verification

1. **1996-2007 quantitative baseline:** Publicly accessible sources are much richer for 2010 onward. A full 1996-2007 series for staffing, facilities, utilisation, maternal mortality, infant mortality and health spending should be sourced from archived state statistical digests, DHIS/HMIS or federal reports.

2. **Facility-count definitions:** Historical state sources mention 293 PHCs, while the 2024 national profile lists 356 public functional PHCs. These should be retained as dated/definition-specific figures until a common methodology is established.

3. **Health-insurance coverage:** The 2024 national profile reports 11.4% / 25,000 coverage, while state programme communications cite much larger numbers of Ulerawa/free-service beneficiaries. These are different concepts (insurance enrolment versus programme beneficiaries) and should not be conflated.

4. **Hospital naming/status:** Some facilities have changed status over time (specialist hospital/general hospital/federal medical centre). A facility master list with historical aliases would improve entity resolution.

5. **FETHI founding date:** The current FETHI website says the hospital was founded in 1959, while other public secondary sources describe a 1998 founding. This discrepancy predates the 1996-2026 window but affects the institution's historical profile and should be resolved from an authoritative institutional archive.

6. **Maternal/infant mortality trend:** Government publications report substantial improvements in the early 2010s, but a continuous 1996-2026 state-level series needs independent statistical validation.

7. **Current 2026 project completion:** Several projects were inspected or approved in 2026 but are not yet documented as completed. Treat them as ongoing/approved, not completed.

8. **Private-sector health system:** The package focuses mainly on public systems because official public documentation is substantially stronger. A full private-hospital/provider directory requires additional licensing and facility-registry sources.

9. **Traditional/complementary medicine:** The Ministry's mandate includes traditional medicine practice, but this package does not yet contain a 30-year evidence timeline for regulation, practitioners, facilities or programmes.

10. **Mental health:** Mental-health services are represented institutionally through tertiary-care departments, but a dedicated 30-year state programme and facility/service-capacity timeline needs deeper primary-source research.

11. **Dental health:** The ministry's mandate and EKSUTH's clinical structure include dentistry, but the package lacks a full state-wide dental service history.

12. **Laboratory and blood services:** The package captures diagnostics and blood-bank references but needs a dedicated timeline for laboratory accreditation, blood collection, transfusion services and public-health laboratory capacity.

13. **Geospatial facility map:** A machine-readable list of every PHC/general hospital/tertiary facility with coordinates would substantially improve the KB.

14. **Yoruba/local-language review:** Community-health programme descriptions and local facility names should receive a local-language/editorial review before publication.
