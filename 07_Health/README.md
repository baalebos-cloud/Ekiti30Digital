# Ekiti State Health Sector Knowledge Base Package

## Scope
A research-oriented knowledge-base package covering the evolution of the Ekiti State health sector from the creation of Ekiti State in 1996 through September 2026.

The package deliberately separates:
- historical timeline entries,
- health institutions,
- programmes and financing mechanisms,
- public-health and clinical subsectors,
- current and ongoing projects,
- quantitative snapshots,
- evidence gaps and disputed/uncertain claims,
- source/manifest metadata.

## Coverage
The research spans:
1. Health-sector governance and policy
2. Primary health care and PHCDA
3. Secondary hospitals and Hospitals Management Board
4. Tertiary care and EKSUTH
5. Federal tertiary institutions serving Ekiti (FETHI and FMC Ikole)
6. Maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health
7. Immunisation and nutrition
8. Malaria and other communicable diseases
9. HIV/AIDS, TB and STI services
10. Lassa fever, Ebola/COVID-19 and emergency preparedness
11. Non-communicable diseases, cancer, sickle-cell and chronic disease care
12. Emergency/ambulance services
13. Medicines, pharmacy and the Drug Management Agency
14. Health insurance/UHC, BHCPF and Ulerawa
15. Health workforce, nursing, midwifery and medical education
16. Diagnostics, laboratories, blood services and specialist care
17. Digital health, surveillance and health data
18. Disability and vulnerable-population access
19. Current infrastructure and ongoing 2024-2026 projects
20. 2026 budget/performance context

## Important evidence rule
Government claims, institutional claims, independent reporting and statistical sources are kept distinguishable. Where figures conflict, the package records the conflict rather than silently selecting a preferred number.

## Date cut-off
Research updated through 25 September 2026.
