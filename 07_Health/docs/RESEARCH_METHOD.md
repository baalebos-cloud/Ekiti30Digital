# Research Method

## Research window
1996-09-25 through 2026-09-25.

## Source hierarchy
Priority was given to:
1. Ekiti State Government official publications, laws, budgets and executive-council decisions.
2. Official hospital/institution websites.
3. Federal Ministry of Health / NCDC / related official federal sources.
4. Reputable Nigerian news outlets for independently reported historical context.
5. Secondary sources only where primary material was not readily available.

## Treatment of claims
- A government statement is labelled as a government-reported outcome where appropriate.
- Current projects are treated as ongoing only when an official source identifies them as ongoing, under construction, approved, or inspected.
- Historical claims that are clearly advocacy/scorecard language are not rewritten as independently verified facts.
- Conflicting statistics are recorded in GAPS.md.

## Current-status rule
The package's "current" section is a snapshot as of 25 September 2026. Projects can change status after this date.
