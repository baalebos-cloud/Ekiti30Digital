# 2026 Current-System Snapshot

The 2026 Q1 Budget Implementation Report lists primary-health expenditure programmes including:
- Effective governance of the health system
- Essential Package of Health Services
- Modern health infrastructure

The report gives an original 2026 primary-health budget of ₦811,091,805.23 and Q1 performance of ₦95,512,071.14 for the programme classification shown.

A February 2026 state-information article reported that the state spent over ₦33.7 billion on health in 2025, with more than half invested in PHC, citing Ulerawa payments, renovation of 103 PHCs, commodities and health-worker incentives as major drivers.

Sources:

Caution: Different budget documents use different classifications and scopes. Do not compare programme-level PHC figures directly with total-sector expenditure without reconciling definitions.
