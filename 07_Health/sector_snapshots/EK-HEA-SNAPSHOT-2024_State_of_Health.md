# 2024 State of Health Snapshot

The Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare's State of Health of the Nation 2024 profile for Ekiti reports:
- Population: 3,836,751
- 2024 state budget share allocated to health: 6.78% / ₦10.82 billion
- State health insurance coverage: 11.4% / 25,000 (as reported in the profile)
- Annual BHCPF disbursement: ₦898,068,895
- 13 implementing partners listed
- HPV vaccination: 210,619 girls aged 9-14, 91% coverage
- HIV treatment: 93%
- Vitamin A: 600,603 supplements, 81% coverage for children 6-59 months
- Public/private functional PHC facilities listed: 356 public and 75 private

Source:
https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/The-State-of-Health-of-the-Nation-Report-2024.pdf

Note: These figures are reproduced as a dated national profile snapshot and should not be treated as the live 2026 position.
