# Health Facility Landscape

The official Hospitals Management Board describes 20 state-owned secondary health facilities under its remit.

The 2024 national State of Health profile lists 356 public and 75 private functional PHC facilities.

Major tertiary/referral institutions relevant to Ekiti include:
- EKSUTH, Ado-Ekiti
- Federal Teaching Hospital, Ido-Ekiti
- Federal Medical Centre, Ikole-Ekiti (converted in 2024)

Important historical note:
A 2012 state publication referred to 293 primary health centres, while the 2024 national profile reports 356 public functional PHC facilities. These figures are not necessarily contradictory because they may reflect different years, definitions and facility classifications.
