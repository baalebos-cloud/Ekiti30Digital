# Free Health Missions / Ulera Ekiti / Ilera Ayo
Mobile and outreach health programmes have been a recurring state strategy since at least the 2010-2014 period, targeting people who face financial or geographic barriers. The model has included general screening/treatment, referrals, eye services and support for vulnerable groups.
