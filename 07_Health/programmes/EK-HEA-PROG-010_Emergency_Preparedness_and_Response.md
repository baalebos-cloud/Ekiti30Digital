# Emergency preparedness and response
Ekiti has developed emergency-response capacity through ambulances, disease-surveillance teams, an Emergency Operations Center, hotlines and outbreak-response structures. COVID-19 provides the clearest documented example, while the state also references responses to Lassa fever and other outbreaks.
