# Digital health, surveillance and health data
The state has used health-data systems, data-collection motorcycles, disease surveillance and GIS-based COVID response. The 2017-2018 medical statistics digest provides a historical data baseline, while current HOPE and budget systems increasingly publish implementation documents online.
