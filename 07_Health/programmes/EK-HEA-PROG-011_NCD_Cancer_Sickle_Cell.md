# Non-communicable diseases, cancer and sickle-cell care
The sector includes NCD prevention, cancer screening and specialist chronic-care services. Earlier programmes included the Funmi Adunni Olayinka Diagnostic and Wellness Centre and free breast/prostate screening; recent policy has included a sickle-cell framework and free insurance enrolment for vulnerable people.
