# Medicines and commodity security
Drug supply has evolved from central medical stores and drug revolving-fund approaches toward a dedicated Drug Management Agency and pharma-grade warehouse. The state reports improved regular access to essential medicines and explicitly links the current system to reducing stock-outs and fake/substandard medicines.
