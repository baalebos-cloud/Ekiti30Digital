# HIV/AIDS, TB and STI services
The state's health system includes HIV/STI commodities, treatment and prevention activities. The 2025 budget estimates include procurement of HIV/AIDS and STI commodities, while the 2024 national state-health profile reports 93% HIV-treatment coverage.
