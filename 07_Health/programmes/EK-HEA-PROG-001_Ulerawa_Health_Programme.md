# Ulerawa Health Programme
Ulerawa is an Ekiti health-insurance/UHC intervention focused on high-impact primary care. Its package includes antenatal care, delivery, postnatal care, child care, family planning and malaria treatment, with the state reporting broad use across its PHC network.
