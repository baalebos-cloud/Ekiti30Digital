# Ulerawa Health Programme

## Summary

Ulerawa is an Ekiti State health intervention focused on expanding access to high-impact primary healthcare services, particularly maternal and child health services.

## Facts

- The Ekiti State Health Insurance Scheme identifies Ulerawa as one of its major intervention programmes and describes it as a case-bundled system aimed at universal health coverage, particularly for vulnerable populations. [S1]
- Ulerawa's service package includes antenatal care, delivery services, postnatal care, childcare, family planning and malaria treatment. [S1]
- The Ekiti State Health Insurance Scheme reports that Ulerawa expanded to 177 facilities across all 16 local government areas in 2023. [S1]
- The official health-insurance page reports that more than 536,000 services had been provided through core healthcare areas as of March 2025. [S1]
- An Ekiti State Government article dated 18 July 2024 reported that more than 429,000 residents had accessed free medical services through the Ulerawa Health Insurance Programme and said services were being provided across 177 health centres. [S2]

## Evidence Status

The programme descriptions and numerical figures are government-reported information. Numerical claims retain their stated reporting dates and should not be presented as timeless current totals.
