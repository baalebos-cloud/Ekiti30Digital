# Federal Medical Centre, Ikole-Ekiti
The former State Specialist Hospital Ikole was converted to a Federal Medical Centre following a July 2024 MoU between the Federal and Ekiti governments. The institution reports specialist services including orthopaedics, surgery, ophthalmology, paediatrics, internal medicine and other clinics.
