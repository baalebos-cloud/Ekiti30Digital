# Ekiti State University Teaching Hospital (EKSUTH)
EKSUTH is the state's flagship teaching/tertiary hospital. It was established in law in April 2008 from the former State Specialist Hospital, Ado-Ekiti. It provides tertiary care, specialist services, residency training and clinical teaching linked to the EKSU College of Medicine.
