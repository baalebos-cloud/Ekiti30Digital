# Ministry of Health and Human Services
The ministry is the central state policy and regulatory institution for health. Its stated functions include health-policy formulation and implementation, oversight of public and private hospitals and health-related professional institutions, liaison with nursing/midwifery regulators, and infectious-disease prevention. The current structure includes directors/functions covering public health, hospital services/training, nursing, pharmacy, planning, procurement and related agencies.
