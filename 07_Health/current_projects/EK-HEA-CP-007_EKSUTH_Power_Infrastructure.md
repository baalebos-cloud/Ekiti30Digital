# EKSUTH power infrastructure
In July/August 2026 the state approved power interventions for EKSUTH, including replacement/upgrading of a transformer and later installation of two 1MVA transformers and related accessories for the 80-bed facility. The stated objective is uninterrupted power for critical areas including emergency, ICU and maternity services.
