# Ikogosi, Awo and Ipao hospital expansion
- **Status:** active approved construction programme as of 23 September 2026.
- **Background:** in October 2025, PHC facilities at Ikogosi, Awo and Ipao were approved for upgrading to General Hospitals.
- **2026:** State Executive Council approved ₦6.765887612 billion for new hospital buildings at the three locations, ₦2.25529587074 billion each.
- **Target:** 10-month completion.
- **Facilities listed:** wards, mortuary, maternity/delivery suite, children's ward, theatre, laboratory, consulting room, staff quarters and administration.
