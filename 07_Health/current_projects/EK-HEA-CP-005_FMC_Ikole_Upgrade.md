# Federal Medical Centre Ikole upgrade
The 2024 MoU converted the former State Specialist Hospital into a Federal Medical Centre. The upgrade is intended to expand comprehensive specialist care, training and research and reduce the need for residents to travel outside the area for tertiary services.
