# EKSUTH 80-bed multipurpose facility
- **Status as of 25 Sep 2026:** ongoing / under advanced construction and enabling works.
- **Started:** foundation/flag-off in October 2024.
- **Scope:** 80-bed multipurpose medical building.
- **2026 enabling works:** dedicated electrical infrastructure/transformers and water-storage sump were approved to enable occupation and operation.
- **Latest evidence:** Governor inspected the facility on 7 September 2026; August 2026 approvals addressed water supply and electrical infrastructure.
