# Nine general hospitals renovation, solar and equipment programme
- **Facilities:** Ijan, Ijesa-Isu, Ilupeju/Itapa, Ayede, Iye, Ifaki, Ilawe, Efon Alaaye and Oke-Imesi.
- **2024 approval:** major renovation/equipment programme including Government House Clinic, HMB headquarters and selected EKSUTH works.
- **2026 follow-on:** ₦737.743 million approved for solar installation and additional renovation works.
- **Scope examples:** theatres, roofs, ceilings, drainage, accident/emergency unit, air-conditioning and structural repairs.
