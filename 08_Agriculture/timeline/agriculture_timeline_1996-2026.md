### EK-AGR-002
**Claim:** Ikun Dairy Farm, in Ikun-Ekiti (Moba LGA), was established in 1980 under
President Shehu Shagari's administration and the Benin-Owena River Basin Development
Authority, intended to boost the local economy and supply evaporated milk to the
old Ondo State. It then sat largely moribund for roughly 40 years, surviving the
1996 transition into Ekiti State without becoming operational at scale.
**Status:** VERIFIED
**Tier:** B (press account, corroborated by later government/PPP sources citing "40
years moribund")
**Source URL:** https://allafrica.com/stories/201002170166.html
**Notes:** Pre-1996 founding; became an Ekiti State asset upon state creation.
Cross-reference: EK-AGR-010 (2019 revival), institutions/ikun-dairy-farm/.

### EK-AGR-003
**Claim:** The Ekiti State Agricultural Development Programme (ADP) traces to the
Ekiti-Akoko Agricultural Development Project (est. August 1981, covering five LGAs
of the old Ondo State), which became the whole-of-state Ondo State ADP in 1989, and
was reconstituted as the Ekiti State ADP on 1 October 1996 when Ekiti State was
created, headquartered in Ikole-Ekiti.
**Status:** VERIFIED
**Tier:** B (peer-reviewed/academic source, corroborated by government MDA listing)
**Source URL:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/273035534_Impacts_of_Agricultural_Development_Programme_ADP_on_Rural_Dwellers_in_Nigeria_A_Study_of_Isan-Ekiti
**Notes:** Cross-reference: institutions/ekiti-adp/.

### EK-AGR-007
**Claim:** In July 2019, Ekiti State signed a public-private partnership roadmap
with Promasidor Nigeria Limited to resuscitate Ikun Dairy Farm; land clearing and
farmhouse renovation began October 2019, with the Central Bank of Nigeria
contributing to a $5 million investment package alongside Promasidor.
**Status:** VERIFIED
**Tier:** A/B (government MDA press releases + Businessday + NIPC)
**Source URLs:**
- https://www.ekitistate.gov.ng/archives/22727
- https://www.nipc.gov.ng/2019/12/23/ekiti-promasidor-reactivate-ikun-dairy-farm-with-400-cows/
- https://www.ekitistate.gov.ng/archives/22817
**Notes:** Sources disagree on the initial cattle count — one government press
release cites 227 cows "from the United States," another (NIPC, reproducing a
Promasidor executive's statement) cites 400 cows acquired for the first stage. Not
resolved this pass; do not ingest a specific initial headcount as VERIFIED without
reconciling. Cross-reference: EK-AGR-002, EK-AGR-015, institutions/ikun-dairy-farm/.

### EK-AGR-009
**Claim:** The Ekiti Agro-Allied International Cargo Airport — described by
government sources as the first airport in Nigeria conceived primarily around
agricultural export logistics — was initiated under Governor Fayemi (site handover
to contractor for the 3.2km runway reported by 2020), continued under Governor
Oyebanji, received Federal Government approval for commercial flight operations in
October 2025, and was formally commissioned in December 2025.
**Status:** VERIFIED (existence, phased construction, and commercial-ops approval
independently corroborated across government and press sources); commissioning date
ATTRIBUTED to a single opinion-piece account and should be checked against an
official commissioning announcement
**Tier:** B
**Source URLs:**
- https://www.ekitistate.gov.ng/archives/23873
- https://nigeriastories.com/2025/10/07/fg-approves-commercial-flight-operations-at-ekiti-agro-allied-international-cargo-airport/
- https://kolaking.substack.com/p/ekiti-airport-ready-for-commercial
**Notes:** A December 2025 Tribune opinion piece raises an open critique — as of
that writing, no agricultural cargo shipments, cold-storage, or quarantine
infrastructure had reportedly been observed at the site despite "over 20 landings."
Flagged in Gaps as a live, unresolved tension between the project's stated purpose
and its documented functionality. Cross-reference:
institutions/agro-allied-cargo-airport/.

and began field distribution of hybrid seedlings by mid-2026.
**Status:** VERIFIED
**Tier:** B
**Source URLs:**
- https://leadership.ng/ekiti-moves-to-raise-10m-cocoa-seedlings-in-10-years/
