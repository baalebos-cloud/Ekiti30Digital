# Agriculture -- Gaps and Follow-ups
(Naming matches the `06_Education/Education_Gaps_and_Follow-ups.md` convention.)

1. No systematic time-series data (annual yield, farmer income, hectares under
   cultivation, statewide employment) across 1996-2026 -- everything gathered this
   pass is programme/project-level, sourced to press releases and one-off
   announcements. Needs direct engagement with the Ministry of Agriculture and
   Food Security's Planning, Research & Statistics directorate or National Bureau
   of Statistics agriculture surveys.
2. The 2017 "70 million cocoa seedlings" target (EK-AGR-005) and the 2026 "10
   million cocoa seedlings" target (EK-AGR-014) are not reconciled -- unclear
   whether the first programme lapsed, was renamed, or ran in parallel. Needs a
   direct query to the Ministry's Tree Crops department.
3. Ikun Dairy Farm's initial 2019 cattle count is disputed across sources (227 vs.
   400 head) -- not resolved this pass.
4. The Agro-Allied Cargo Airport's December 2025 commissioning date rests on a
   single opinion-piece account, not an official commissioning announcement --
   needs a primary government source. Separately, a December 2025 Tribune opinion
   piece raises a live, unresolved critique that the airport lacks the
   warehouse/cold-storage/quarantine infrastructure its "agro-allied" framing
   implies -- this is a genuine open dispute, not a settled fact either way, and
   should be presented as such rather than resolved in either direction without
   further sourcing.
5. YCAD's founding (EK-AGR-006) rests entirely on a chain of academic secondary
   citations (2023 paper citing a 2017 paper citing an unlocated 2013 "ADP
   overview" document) -- no primary 2011 policy document was found. Needs a
   direct source check.
6. The CBN-ABP rice programme's 62,000-jobs target (EK-AGR-008) has no located
   follow-up reporting on outcomes -- current status as of 2026 is unknown.
7. Egbeja Snail Village (Okemesi) / "Farmkonnect" snail farm -- mentioned in
   passing in a CBN-ABP press release (annual production of 3.6 million snails,
   described as the largest such operation) but not independently researched or
   written up this pass. Candidate for a future institutions/ entry.
8. The Ministry's own MDA page URL slug reads "agriculture-and-rural-development"
   while all 2023-2026 press coverage uses "Ministry of Agriculture and Food
   Security" -- likely an unreflected rename, not resolved (EK-AGR-017 notes).
9. Farm Settlement scheme history (Ekiti inherited multiple farm settlements from
   the old Western Region/Ondo State era, e.g., the kind of settlement referenced
   in the Ministry's "Farm Settlement and Peasant Farmers Development"
   directorate) -- pre-1996 institutional history not researched this pass.
10. None of this content has been through Yoruba review (per the Education
    section's precedent, Member 1/Faith Ogunlade was named as reviewer there --
    unconfirmed whether the same applies here) -- required before `ingestible:
    yes` per the PR #11 Part C launch-language decision referenced in Education.
11. **Ownership/placement unresolved** -- unlike Education, no repo member or
    Issue number is known to correspond to this content. See README's "Open
    question" section.
