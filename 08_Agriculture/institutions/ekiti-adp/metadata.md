# Ekiti State Agricultural Development Programme (ADP)
- Lineage: Ekiti-Akoko Agricultural Development Project (est. Aug 1981, old Ondo
  State, 5 LGAs) → whole-of-state Ondo State ADP (1989) → Ekiti State ADP
  (reconstituted 1 Oct 1996 on state creation).
- Headquartered in Ikole-Ekiti.
- Follows the national ADP model (Training & Visit extension system), first
  launched nationally in 1972, aimed at raising smallholder food production and
  farmer incomes via inputs, credit access, and rural infrastructure.
- Academic case study (Isan-Ekiti, Oye LGA) found ADP provision of improved seeds,
  fertiliser, and pesticides had a positive impact on production, but that credit
  accessibility showed no significant effect on productivity — a funding-access gap
  flagged in the literature, not resolved.
- Current structure includes a Programme Manager role (per Ministry MDA listing)
  and is one of three listed parastatals/units under the Ministry of Agriculture
  and Food Security, alongside Fountain Agricultural Marketing Agency and the
  Directorate of Farm Settlement/Peasant Farmers Development.
- One of three sites selected for L-PRES agro-meteorological stations (2026),
  representing the Derived Savannah agro-ecological zone.
