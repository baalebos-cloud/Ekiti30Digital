# Ekiti Agro-Allied International Cargo Airport, Ado-Ekiti
- Conceived under Governor Kayode Fayemi as a flagship "legacy project," explicitly
  framed by Fayemi as more important to him than the state's other infrastructure
  projects, intended to give the landlocked state direct export access for
  agricultural produce.
- Site handover to contractor for the 3.2km runway reported as underway by 2020;
  construction continued across the Fayemi and Oyebanji administrations.
- Described in government and press sources as the first airport in Nigeria
  conceived primarily around agro-allied cargo logistics rather than passenger
  traffic — an "aerotropolis" concept tied to the adjacent Ekiti Knowledge Zone and
  Agriculture Processing Zone (silo, juice factory, Dangote Mill, GMK Mill cited as
  co-located or planned).
- Federal Government approval for commercial flight operations reported October
  2025; formal commissioning reported December 2025 (single-source account, flagged
  for confirmation — see Gaps).
- **Open critique, unresolved:** a December 2025 opinion piece in the Nigerian
  Tribune states that despite "over 20 landings and take-offs" since commissioning,
  no agricultural cargo shipments, warehouse, cargo-screening, or cold-storage
  facility had been observed on site — only the terminal building. This directly
  contradicts the airport's stated purpose and should be treated as a live,
  disputed claim rather than settled history.
