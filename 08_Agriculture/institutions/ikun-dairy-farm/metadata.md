# Ikun Dairy Farm, Ikun-Ekiti (Moba LGA)
- Established 1980 under President Shehu Shagari and the Benin-Owena River Basin
  Development Authority, intended to supply evaporated milk locally and boost the
  community's economy. Survived the 1996 creation of Ekiti State as a state asset
  but remained largely moribund for roughly 40 years across multiple
  administrations — a widely-repeated "40 years abandoned" framing appears
  consistently across sources spanning 2010 to 2026.
- **Revival (2019–present):** Ekiti State Government entered a public-private
  partnership with Promasidor Nigeria Limited (makers of Cowbell/Loya milk brands)
  in July 2019 under Governor Fayemi; land clearing began October 2019; the Central
  Bank of Nigeria contributed to a $5 million joint investment package.
- Sources disagree on the initial cattle count at reactivation (227 vs. 400 head —
  see timeline EK-AGR-007 notes). Not resolved this pass.
- By 2025/2026: 500+ hectares, 750+ high-yielding cattle, described by Promasidor
  as Nigeria's largest dairy farm; >80,000 litres of milk/month against a stated
  10,000 litres/day full-capacity target; >200 direct jobs, 1,000+ indirect jobs
  claimed (Promasidor's own account). Promasidor holds a reported 76% equity stake.
- Governor Fayemi described the farm as a potential prototype for the National
  Livestock Transformation Plan, addressing farmer–herder conflict nationally.
- Cross-reference: EK-AGR-002, EK-AGR-007, EK-AGR-010 (timeline).
