# Tractorisation Support Scheme

Launched 2023 under Governor Biodun Oyebanji. The Ekiti State Executive Council
approves an annual subsidy (50% as of the 2026 planting season) on tractorisation
services — ploughing, harrowing, and ridging — for farmers ahead of each planting
season.

Per Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security Ebenezer Boluwade (2026 planting
season announcement):
- 2026 marks the fourth consecutive year of the subsidy.
- Farmers pay only after confirmation that their land is suitable for
  tractorisation.
- Service providers are expected to mobilise to project sites within 48 hours of
  confirmation.
- Farmers contact designated coordinators by senatorial district.
- The Commissioner attributed a slight year-on-year price increase to rising
  diesel costs, linked to global tensions affecting fuel markets (Iran/US/Israel),
  with prices flagged as subject to downward review.
