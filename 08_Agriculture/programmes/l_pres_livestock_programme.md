# Livestock Productivity and Resilience Support (L-PRES)

A World Bank-supported project operating in Ekiti State under the Oyebanji
administration, coordinated through a dedicated State Project Coordinator and
Project Implementation Unit within the Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security.
Governance structure (per the Ministry's own MDA page) includes a State Steering
Committee chaired by the Commissioner for Agriculture and Food Security, with
membership from Infrastructure & Public Utilities, Finance, Trade/Investment/
Cooperatives, Women Affairs, and the Special Adviser on Security.

Documented activities:
- Livestock infrastructure installed/inspected across Ijero-Ekiti, Ikere-Ekiti,
  and Ilawe-Ekiti, aimed at strengthening the livestock value chain and hygienic
  meat processing.
- Three agro-meteorological stations approved (2026), sited to cover the state's
  three agro-ecological zones: Ministry of Agriculture complex (Ado-Ekiti,
  Forest zone), ADP complex (Ikole-Ekiti), and BOUESTI Faculty of Agriculture
  (Ikere-Ekiti, completing Derived Savannah/Savannah coverage per the
  Commissioner's account).
- Reference in the Ministry's MDA listing to planned/existing infrastructure
  including a Livestock Development Center (Ado-Ekiti), Irele grazing reserve, and
  the old Oke-Ako cattle ranch.
- The poultry/broiler scheme (see poultry_broiler_scheme.md) operates through
  L-PRES's Ekiti coordination office.
