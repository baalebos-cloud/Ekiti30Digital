# Youth Commercial Agricultural Development (YCAD)

Established in 2011 under Governor Kayode Fayemi's first term as one leg of a
three-part agricultural framework (alongside public-private partnerships and
agricultural subsidies) designed to address youth unemployment — cited at over 52%
in a 2013 state ADP overview.

Per academic secondary literature (Ajayi, 2017, as cited in a 2023 ResearchGate
paper), YCAD has worked to empower youth through:
- Provision of land and agricultural equipment
- Milk collection support
- Seed multiplication and capacity building
- Dairy development
- Cocoa development
- Other agricultural value-chain interventions

**Status of sourcing:** This entire write-up rests on a single academic secondary
source (Weinoh, 2023) citing an earlier academic source (Ajayi, 2017) which in turn
cites a 2013 "Ekiti State ADP overview" document not independently located this
pass. No primary state government policy document establishing YCAD was found. This
should be treated as ATTRIBUTED pending a primary source check — see Gaps.
