# Ekiti Broiler Production Scheme / Poultry Programme

Run through the L-PRES project's Ekiti State coordination office, under Commissioner
Ebenezer Boluwade. Stated aim: position Ekiti among Nigeria's leading poultry
(chicken-market) producing states, via expanded youth training, new processing
infrastructure, and wider livestock-farmer support.

Documented figures (2026, single press source — not independently corroborated):
- Six additional broiler rearing centres established statewide.
- Three farmer field schools established across the state's senatorial districts
  for ongoing technical training.
- 200+ young people trained across four separate training batches.
- 150,000+ birds produced through the scheme sold, both within Ekiti and elsewhere
  in Nigeria.
- A poultry processing facility reported as approved (not confirmed operational).
