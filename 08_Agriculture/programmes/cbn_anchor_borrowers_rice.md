# CBN Anchor Borrowers' Programme (ABP) — Rice Financing in Ekiti State

Under Governor Fayemi, Ekiti State partnered with the Central Bank of Nigeria's
national Anchor Borrowers' Programme, which provided input financing to smallholder
farmers via commodity associations ("anchors"). Then-CBN Governor Godwin Emefiele
is quoted (in a state government press release covering a joint appearance with
Fayemi) citing:
- A target of 62,000 jobs created through CBN-ABP rice farming in Ekiti State
- CBN support extending beyond rice to palm, cocoa, and cassava cultivation
  statewide
- CBN's $5 million contribution to the Ikun Dairy Farm PPP (see
  institutions/ikun-dairy-farm/) as a related but separate investment
- "Massive rice pyramids" launched in the state as visible evidence of ABP rice
  output

**Sourcing caveat:** The 62,000-jobs figure comes from a single government press
release reproducing the CBN Governor's remarks at a joint event; it is a stated
target/claim, not an audited outcome. No later source in this pass reports on
whether the target was met, partially met, or abandoned — Ekiti's ABP rice
programme's current status (as of 2026) is not documented in the sources gathered
this pass. Flagged as a research gap, not resolved.
