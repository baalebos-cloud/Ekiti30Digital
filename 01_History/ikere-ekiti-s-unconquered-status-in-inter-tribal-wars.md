# Ikere-Ekiti's Unconquered Status in Inter-tribal Wars

## Traditional / Community Account

Ikere-Ekiti is traditionally described as the only town in Ekiti never conquered during the region's 19th-century inter-tribal wars, a status locally credited in part to the protective power of the Olosunta and Orole hill deities.
