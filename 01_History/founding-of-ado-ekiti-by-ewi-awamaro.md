# Founding of Ado-Ekiti by Ewi Awamaro

## Traditional / Community Account

Oral tradition holds that Ado-Ekiti was founded by Ewi Awamaro, a prince originally from Ile-Ife, who migrated west (via Oba-Ile, Ido-Ani and Agbado) and settled at the site of an existing community called Ulesun/Ilesun, ruled by Elesun, which he took over and renamed Ado. Founding-date estimates vary by source (12th vs. 14th century) and should be treated as unresolved.
