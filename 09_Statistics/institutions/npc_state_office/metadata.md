name: National Population Commission (NPC), Ekiti State Office
type: Federal statutory agency, state office
---
The NPC state office is the federal body responsible for census enumeration and vital-registration statistics within Ekiti State, and is the ultimate source of the state's 1991 and 2006 census figures (the only full national headcounts conducted since the state's creation) — see EK-STAT-001 and EK-STAT-003. Post-2006 population figures used across this knowledge base (including in the Education, Agriculture and Health sector packages) are NPC/NBS growth-rate projections rather than fresh counts, and carry acknowledged wide error margins nationally.
