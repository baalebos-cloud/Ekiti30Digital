SBS is Ekiti's official state statistics authority, responsible for coordinating state-level data collection, analysis and publication (population estimates, sectoral employment data, and open-dataset hosting). Its public-facing "StatCloud" portal offers interactive analytics, a data-collection form for public/agency contributors, and a data warehouse, self-reporting 3.2 million estimated population, 16 LGAs covered, an 84% literacy rate, and 142 open datasets at time of research. This is the natural institutional home for reconciling the population/literacy figure discrepancies flagged elsewhere in this package (EK-STAT-004, EK-STAT-011/012) — a good target for direct outreach if a live contact is available on the technical team.
