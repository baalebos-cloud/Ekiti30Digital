title: SBS StatCloud portal reports 3.2M population estimate and 84% literacy rate
category: Institutions / Population / Literacy
---
The state's own Bureau of Statistics, via its public "StatCloud" portal, cites a current estimated population of 3.2 million across the 16 LGAs and an 84% literacy rate, alongside 142 open datasets covering employment distribution by sector and other indicators. This 84% literacy figure differs materially from a separately sourced 2024/2025 national media estimate crediting Ekiti with a 95.79% literacy rate (see EK-STAT-012) — an unresolved discrepancy, likely reflecting different definitions (e.g. adult vs. youth literacy, or different survey years/methodologies) rather than one source simply being wrong. Flagged in Gaps rather than reconciled.

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — "Ekiti State Bureau of Statistics – StatCloud."
