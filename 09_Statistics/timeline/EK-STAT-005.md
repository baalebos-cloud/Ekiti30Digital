title: NBS 2010 per-capita poverty headcount for Ekiti at 62.6%
category: Poverty / Economy
---
Using a per-capita measurement approach, the National Bureau of Statistics reported a poverty headcount of 62.6% for Ekiti State in 2010 — a figure later cited by academic researchers as a comparison baseline against subsequent, markedly lower poverty measurements (see EK-STAT-008, EK-STAT-010), illustrating both real improvement over time and the sensitivity of poverty statistics to measurement methodology.

**Sources:** Scope Journal — "Measurement of Poverty in Ekiti State, Nigeria" (academic paper citing NBS 2010 data).
