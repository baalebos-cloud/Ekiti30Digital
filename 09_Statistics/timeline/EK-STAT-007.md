title: NBS Nigeria Living Standards Survey (NLSS) — Ekiti poverty headcount at 52.1%
category: Poverty / Economy
---
Drawing on the 2018/2019 National Living Standards Survey (NLSS) per-capita income line (₦137,430/year), the NBS reported Ekiti's poverty headcount, depth and severity at 52.1%, 17.4% and 7.8% respectively. Around the same period, a 2019 NBS-cited characterization described Ekiti as "the poorest [state] in the [South West] region" based on internally generated revenue and related fiscal indicators, distinct from its later (2022) strong multidimensional-poverty ranking — see EK-STAT-008 and Gaps for how these different snapshots relate.

**Sources:** Scope Journal — "Measurement of Poverty in Ekiti State, Nigeria" (citing NBS 2020/NLSS 2018-19 data); academia.edu — "Spatial Distribution of Poverty indicators in Ekiti State" (citing NBS adapted poverty line, 2019).
