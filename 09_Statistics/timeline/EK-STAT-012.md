title: National media analysis ranks Ekiti 3rd-highest literacy rate nationally (95.79%)
category: Literacy / Education

A Guardian Nigeria analysis of national literacy data (2024 estimates) placed Ekiti State's literacy rate at 95.79%, ranked third-highest nationally behind Imo (96.43%) and Lagos (96.3%). This is presented alongside — not reconciled against — the state's own StatCloud figure of 84% (EK-STAT-011); both are retained in this knowledge base as sourced but conflicting data points.

**Sources:** The Guardian (Nigeria) — "What is the Literacy Rate in Nigeria?," 15 Dec 2025.
