title: 2022 National Multidimensional Poverty Index — Ekiti ranks 5th-lowest nationally
category: Poverty / Economy
---
The Federal Government, through NBS (with NASSCO, UNDP, UNICEF and OPHI), launched the 2022 Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) survey — sampling over 56,000 households nationwide between November 2021 and February 2022. Nationally, 63% of Nigerians (133 million people) were found to be multidimensionally poor. Ekiti State ranked 5th-best (i.e., among the states with the lowest poverty) with an MPI of 0.125, covering approximately 36% of its roughly 1.31 million-population estimate as multidimensionally poor — a notable result given the state's comparatively low IGR/FAAC ranking (35th and 32nd nationally, respectively, per the same period's analysis) and its earlier "poorest in the region" characterization (EK-STAT-007). This tension is treated by commentators as a story of good fiscal/programme design outperforming raw financial resources, not resolved statistical contradiction — see Gaps.

**Sources:** National Bureau of Statistics — "Nigeria Launches its Most Extensive National Measure of Multidimensional Poverty," 17 Nov 2022; TheCable — "Multi-dimensional poverty index: How Ekiti is punching above its weight," 4 Jan 2023; OPHI/NBS — "Nigeria Multidimensional Poverty Index (2022)" report.
