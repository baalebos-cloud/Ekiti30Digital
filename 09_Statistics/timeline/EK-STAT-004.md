title: NPC/NBS projection puts Ekiti (and Ekiti-speaking population) at over 3.27 million
category: Population / Projection
---
Population figures beyond the 2006 census are projections, not counts. A cited 2016 NPC/NBS-based projection places Ekiti State's population at 3,270,798, within a wider "Ekiti people" (ethnic/linguistic group, spanning into Ondo, Kwara and Kogi states) total of roughly 4,175,608 for 2011–2016. Because Nigeria's census figures and projection methodologies are widely disputed nationally (citypopulation.de notes "all population figures for Nigeria show high error rates; census results are disputed"), later state-level population figures cited in other sectors of this knowledge base (e.g. "~3.5 million" in the Health sector, "3.2M" per the state's own StatCloud portal) should be treated as approximate and non-reconciled — see Gaps.

**Sources:** Official Records — "Ekiti people"; citypopulation.de — "Ekiti (State, Nigeria)."
