title: Ekiti tops national IGR annual growth rate ranking
category: Fiscal / Economy
---
Ekiti State's Internally Generated Revenue (IGR) rose from ₦17.03 billion in 2022 to ₦29.82 billion in 2023 — a 75% growth rate, the highest annual IGR growth rate of any state in Nigeria for that year, per the Joint Tax Board (JTB)'s ranking presented at its 155th meeting. The state also moved from among the five worst-performing states to 16th position in Total Collections Ranking, and from 33rd to 15th position under Direct Assessment Parameters. State officials attributed the improvement to "proactive measures and policies."

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — "Ekiti Tops IGR Annual Growth Rate Ranking," 4 Jul 2024 (reporting on Joint Tax Board data).
