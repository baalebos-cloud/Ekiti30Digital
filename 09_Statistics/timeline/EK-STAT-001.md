title: 1991 census records 1,647,822 people in the Ekiti area (pre-statehood)
category: Population / Census
---
The 1991 national census recorded the population of the territory that would become Ekiti State at 1,647,822 people, still administered as part of the old Ondo State at that time. This figure is the earliest reliable baseline used by the state government for its own historical population narrative.

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — "About Ekiti"; Naijadirectory — "Ekiti State" profile; Situation Room — "Overview of Ekiti State."
