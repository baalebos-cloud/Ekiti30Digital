title: State creation; population estimated at 1,750,000; 16 LGAs constituted
category: Population / Governance
---
On the creation of Ekiti State on 1 October 1996, its population was officially estimated at 1,750,000, with Ado-Ekiti as capital. The state was constituted with 16 Local Government Areas (LGAs) — the administrative geography still in use today, and the basis for practically all subsequent state-level statistical reporting and LGA-level breakdowns. Several LGAs (e.g. Gbonyin/Ayekire) were themselves newly carved out of older LGA boundaries on this same date.

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — "About Ekiti"; Official Records — "Gbonyin."
