title: 2006 National Population Census — Ekiti at 2,384,212
category: Population / Census
---
The 2006 National Population Census, conducted by the National Population Commission (NPC), recorded Ekiti State's population at 2,384,212 — the last full national headcount available at time of writing (Nigeria has not conducted a subsequent constitutionally recognized census as of this package's compilation; all later state figures are NPC/NBS projections rather than counts — see Gaps and EK-STAT-004). LGA-level 2006 figures are available for individual LGAs (e.g. Ikere-Ekiti: 147,355; Gbonyin: 147,999; Ado-Ekiti: 313,690).

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — "About Ekiti"; citypopulation.de — "Ekiti (State, Nigeria)"; Official Records — "Ikere-Ekiti," "Gbonyin," "Ado Ekiti."
