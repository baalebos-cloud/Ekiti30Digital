Two disputed figures are on record for Ekiti's literacy rate: the state's own StatCloud portal cites **84%**, while a December 2025 Guardian Nigeria analysis of national data places Ekiti at **95.79%**, third-highest of all states behind Imo (96.43%) and Lagos (96.3%). Both figures are retained here rather than resolved — see EK-STAT-011/012 and Gaps. Cross-reference: sector-specific education infrastructure and enrollment detail lives in this repository's 06_Education package, not duplicated here.

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — StatCloud; The Guardian (Nigeria), 15 Dec 2025.
