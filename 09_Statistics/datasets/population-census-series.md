| Year | Population | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1991 | 1,647,822 | National census (pre-statehood territory) |
| 1996 | 1,750,000 (est.) | State-creation estimate |
| 2006 | 2,384,212 | National census (NPC) |
| 2016 | 3,270,798 | NPC/NBS growth-rate projection |
| 2022-2025 (various) | ~3.2M-3.5M (cited across sources) | Projections; unreconciled — see Gaps |

**Caveats:** Nigeria has not held a further constitutionally recognized census since 2006; every figure after 2006 in this table, and everywhere else in this knowledge base, is a projection carrying acknowledged high error margins (per citypopulation.de). LGA-level 2006 figures exist for individual LGAs (e.g. Ado-Ekiti 313,690; Ikere-Ekiti 147,355; Gbonyin 147,999) and could be compiled into a full 16-LGA table in a follow-up pass — flagged in Gaps as not yet fully assembled here.

**Sources:** See EK-STAT-001 through EK-STAT-004 for full citations.
