Ekiti recorded the **lowest labour-force participation rate of any Nigerian state in 2023, at 63.4%**, against a national high of 92.3% (Bauchi). The NBS Labour Force Survey Annual Report 2023 carries dedicated Ekiti state tables (by sex, place of residence, educational level, and age-group) — these were not individually transcribed into this package and are flagged as a follow-up extraction opportunity in Gaps. National context for the same reporting period: Nigeria's overall unemployment rate rose to 5.3% in Q1 2024 (from 5% in Q4 2023); youth (15-24) unemployment was 8.4% in Q1 2024; national employment-to-population ratio was 72.2%.

**Sources:** Nigeria Labour Force Survey Annual Report 2023, National Bureau of Statistics; Arise TV, "Nigeria's Unemployment Rate Rises To 5.3% in Q1 2024," Sep 2024.
