| Year | IGR (₦) | National ranking context |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 17.03 billion | Among the five worst-performing states in Total Collections Ranking (per 2022/early-2023 analysis) |
| 2023 | 29.82 billion | **Highest IGR annual growth rate nationally (75%)**; moved to 16th in Total Collections Ranking; moved from 33rd to 15th in Direct Assessment Parameters |

Separately, at the time of the 2022 MPI analysis, Ekiti ranked 35th nationally by FAAC (federal allocation) receipts and 32nd by IGR — underlining that the 2023 IGR growth, while the fastest in the country, started from a low base.

**Sources:** Ekiti State Government website — "Ekiti Tops IGR Annual Growth Rate Ranking," 4 Jul 2024 (citing Joint Tax Board data); TheCable, 4 Jan 2023 (FAAC/IGR ranking context).
